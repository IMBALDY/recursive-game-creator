All gameplay was newly captured for this film from playable builds. The overview wall begins with development snapshots, then all 48 game tiles play moving gameplay: 45 newly recorded GameCraft projects plus Whitebird, Meme Arena and Twilight Front footage captured for this production. Each game was recorded separately and the clips were composited into the wall. Available versions are labelled individually, including the two available Detective Noir snapshots. Meme Arena uses matched staged starting positions and move requests; Racing Rocket Trials uses original launch scenarios and keyboard input; Whitebird uses actual gameplay input and normal in-game choices. No existing gameplay videos were reused. Earlier Meme Arena snapshots include restored compatible models. Editorial text is English; original in-world signs and native game labels are retained. The main film includes English synthetic narration and synchronized English captions, with music lowered during speech. The three individual game films retain their instrumental underscore. Captions occupy a reserved bottom band so the original game interface remains visible.
